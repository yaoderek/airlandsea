AIR · LAND · SEA

Two commanders. Three lanes. First to 12 victory points.
An unofficial, fan-made clone for playing with a friend.

How to play
  • Each battle, you and your opponent are dealt 6 cards across three lanes: Air, Land and Sea.
  • On your turn: deploy a card face-up to its matching lane (its ability triggers), improvise any card face-down to any lane as a plain strength-2, or withdraw.
  • When both hands are empty, whoever holds the higher total strength in a lane controls it — ties (and empty lanes) go to the player with initiative. Control 2 of 3 lanes to win the battle for 6 VP.
  • Withdrawing concedes the battle, but gives up fewer VP the earlier you quit.
  • Between battles the lanes rotate, initiative swaps, and everything is reshuffled. First to 12 VP wins the war.
